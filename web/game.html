THE EGG

A qubit chain is a pan of eggs. Nudge one and the nudge spreads to the rest: that's scrambling. Serve before your golden eggs lose their echo.

How to play
  1. Each level is a chain of 12 qubits, drawn as eggs. The middle one gets nudged (◆).
  2. Pick a pan. Each pan is a different circuit, measured on Moth Atlas with otoc-echo-v1.
  3. The clock runs through 32 echo steps. Eggs blur, crack and scramble as their measured echo |F| fades.
  4. Tap SERVE (or press space) once the cook bar is green, while the golden eggs' echo is above the line.
  5. Too early: raw. Echo too low: scrambled. Too late: burnt. You get three lives.

Measure your own pan on Atlas

Runs otoc-echo-v1 live (12 qubits, depth 32, aer emulator, 1 credit) with your own key. The key stays in this tab and is only sent to the Atlas API.
