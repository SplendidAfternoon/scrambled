THE EGG

Twelve qubits, drawn as a pan of eggs. Crack one and the crack spreads to the rest: that is quantum scrambling, measured for real on Moth Atlas. Serve before your golden eggs scramble.

How to play
  1. The egg with the red ring gets cracked. Protect the eggs with the golden rings.
  2. Pick a pan. Each one spreads the crack differently, so read the descriptions.
  3. Cooking starts at once. The rings turn green and the button says SERVE NOW when serving would win.
  4. Tap SERVE, press space or tap the pan. Too early: raw. Too late: scrambled or burnt. You have three lives.
