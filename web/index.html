How it works

  1. 1

    Nudge

    A chain of twelve qubits runs forward in time, and the middle qubit gets a small kick.

  2. 2

    Rewind

    The chain runs backwards. If nothing spread, every qubit returns to where it started.

  3. 3

    Measure

    Atlas measures how much of the kick each qubit still carries at every step. When the dynamics scramble, it fades.

  4. 4

    Cook

    Each qubit becomes a strip of egg, an echo tap or a band of the plugin's shell, so you see and hear the fading.

All runs use Atlas's aer emulator with exact expectation values; no quantum hardware was used. Images come from Atlas's blur-v1 and telablur-v1; cutting them into strips is classical compositing. Every job id is in the repository.
